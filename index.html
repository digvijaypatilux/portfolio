◦ About

Hey, nice to meet you.

My journey into design started after college, when I began learning graphic design and exploring branding, freelance projects, and visual communication. As I gained experience, I started working on websites, email campaigns, and social media creatives, which gradually led me toward UI/UX and product design.

With 5+ years of experience in design, including 3+ years in UI/UX and product design, I now work on consumer and enterprise products.
